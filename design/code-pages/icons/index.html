OrhanIcons.Spark and the custom set

The design defines 46 symbols. 41 of them are Material Symbols redrawn in the Orhan house style (24dp, 1.9 stroke) and are not duplicated here — use Icons.Rounded.* so the app keeps tracking upstream. The remaining 5 are drawn for this product and have no Material equivalent. Those are ported to ImageVector, with every pathData string copied verbatim from design/index.html.
